What is こめんとみまもり Live?

こめんとみまもり Live is a Google Chrome extension that adds a pause before you read YouTube comments containing wording that may be distressing.

You choose whether to view a comment.
The extension is not intended to judge a commenter’s character, censor opinions, or penalize anyone.

Main features

The following features are currently implemented primarily for regular messages in YouTube Live Chat.

  • Standard Pause: places a pause over comments that match the 13 built-in standard terms.
  • Words I’d Rather Avoid: lets you save words or phrases, turn matching for all saved words ON/OFF with the Master switch, and turn each word ON/OFF individually.
  • View Comment (Manual Reveal): lets you choose to view the individual comment behind a pause.
  • Export, Import, and Reset: backs up saved words and their ON/OFF settings, restores them by replacing the whole set, or resets them to their defaults.
  • Japanese and English: supports the Popup, Options, and Live Chat pause interface.
    This does not translate comments or your saved words.

Turning Master OFF stops matching for your saved words.
Standard rules remain active independently of the Master and individual word switches.

See the User Guide for basic steps and the Privacy Policy for information about data handling.

Current scope

YouTube Live Chat

The main focus is regular Live Chat messages in viewing pages, YouTube Studio, and pop-out windows.
These support combined matching with the 13 standard terms and your saved words, plus a Japanese or English pause interface.

Video comments and Studio’s video comments page

Top-level comments have a technical proof of concept (PoC) for hiding comment text before checking it, called Protect-first.
They do not support Live Chat’s combined matching with the 13 standard terms and saved words, or its display language switching.
They cannot be used as an equivalent to the Live Chat protection features.

Outside the scope or not yet verified

Replies, Super Chat, Super Stickers, membership notifications, and other message types are outside the scope or unverified.
Protection has not been verified for every display path, including changes to how messages are displayed during high-volume chats.

Limitations and notes

  • This is a prototype in development.
    It is not published in the Chrome Web Store.
  • Rule-based matching can produce false positives and miss comments.
  • Changes to YouTube’s page structure may prevent the extension from working correctly.
  • It does not guarantee complete safety or protection for every comment and display path.

When changing saved-word settings, follow the steps for closing all Live Chats before making changes.
