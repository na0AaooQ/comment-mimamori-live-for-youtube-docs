What is こめんとみまもり Live?

こめんとみまもり Live is a Google Chrome extension that adds a pause before you read YouTube comments containing wording that may be distressing.

You choose whether to view a comment.
The extension is not intended to judge a commenter’s character, censor opinions, or penalize anyone.

Main features

The extension adds a pause for supported YouTube live stream comments (Live Chat), regular video comments, and video comments in YouTube Studio.
The standard rules currently check for 13 strong expressions that may be distressing.
The same rules check for enabled words or phrases you’ve saved.
A pause appears when a comment matches a standard term or an enabled saved term.
Protect-first means hiding comment text before checking it.
Within supported page layouts, the extension leaves text hidden while it checks a comment or if the check fails.

  • Comment display: shows comments marked SAFE automatically.
    For comments marked CUSHION, the text stays hidden behind a pause.
  • Words I’d Rather Avoid: lets you save words or phrases, turn matching for all saved words ON/OFF with the Master switch, and turn each word ON/OFF individually.
  • View Comment (Manual Reveal): lets you choose to view the individual comment behind a pause.
  • Export, Import, and Reset: backs up saved words and their ON/OFF settings, restores them by replacing the whole set, or resets them to their defaults.
  • Japanese and English: supports the Popup, Options, and the extension’s interface in Live Chat, regular video comments, and Studio’s video comments pages.
    This does not translate comments or your saved words.

Turning Master OFF stops matching for your saved words.
Standard rules remain active independently of the Master and individual word switches.

See the User Guide for basic steps and the Privacy Policy for information about data handling.

Current scope

YouTube Live Chat

Regular Live Chat messages on viewing pages, in YouTube Studio, and in pop-out windows are supported.

Regular video comments

Supported top-level comments (excluding replies) on regular video pages (/watch and /live) are covered.
When you switch videos through supported in-page navigation, the extension tries to check the current video’s comments and resume automatically.

Video comments in YouTube Studio

Top-level comments on an individual video’s comments page in Studio are supported.
When you switch to another video within Studio, the extension may keep supported comments hidden and ask you to reload the page.

Outside the scope or not yet verified

Replies, Studio’s channel-wide comment management page, Super Chat, Super Stickers, membership notifications, and Shorts are outside the scope.
Protection has yet to be verified for every way comments appear, including unfamiliar page layouts and changes in how large numbers of comments are displayed.

Limitations and notes

  • This is a prototype in development.
    It is not published in the Chrome Web Store.
  • Rule-based matching can produce false positives and miss comments.
  • Changes to YouTube’s page structure may prevent the extension from working correctly.
  • It does not guarantee complete safety or protection for every comment and display path.

When you save changes to your word settings or display language, the extension tries to update open, supported comment sections.
There is no guarantee that every screen will be notified, that updates will complete, or how long they will take.
If changes do not appear, see how to apply saved-word settings.
