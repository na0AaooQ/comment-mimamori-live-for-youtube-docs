What is こめんとみまもり Live?

こめんとみまもり Live is a Google Chrome extension that adds a pause before you read YouTube comments containing abusive or potentially distressing language.

You choose whether to view a comment.
The extension is not intended to judge a commenter’s character, censor opinions, or penalize anyone.

Main features

The extension supports YouTube Live Chat, regular video comments, comments on YouTube Shorts, and comments for individual videos in YouTube Studio.
The extension checks supported comments for 13 standard words and phrases that may be distressing and for saved words or phrases you’ve turned on.
On supported layouts, comment text stays hidden while a check is in progress or if the check cannot finish.

  • Showing comments: comments that do not need a pause appear automatically.
    Comments that need a pause stay hidden behind a notice. Select “View Comment” to reveal only the comment you choose.
  • Words I'd Rather Avoid: you can add, edit, or delete words or phrases that you would prefer to read after a pause.
    You can turn the pause for all your saved words ON or OFF, or change the setting for each word individually.
  • Export, Import, and Reset: back up your saved words and their ON/OFF settings, restore them by replacing the whole set, or reset them to their defaults.
  • Japanese and English: choose Automatic, Japanese, or English for Settings, the pop-up, Live Chat, regular video comments, Shorts, and individual video comments in Studio.
    Comment text and your saved words stay in their original language.

Turning “Pause for Saved Words” OFF stops the pause for your saved words.
Standard Pause continues to check for the 13 standard words and phrases.

See the User Guide for basic steps with images and demonstration videos, and the Privacy Policy for information about data handling.

Current scope

YouTube Live Chat (live streams)

Regular Live Chat messages on viewing pages, in YouTube Studio, and in pop-out windows are supported.

Regular video comments

The extension covers supported comments that are not replies on regular video pages (/watch and /live).
When you switch videos without reloading on a supported page, the extension tries to resume checking comments on the new video.

Comments on YouTube Shorts

The extension adds a pause to comments that are not replies on supported Shorts layouts.

When a comment needs a pause, its content stays hidden. You can select “View Comment” to reveal only that comment.

When you close and reopen the comments panel, the extension checks the comments again using your current settings.

Video comments in YouTube Studio

The extension covers comments that are not replies on an individual video’s comments page in Studio.
When you switch to another video within Studio, the extension may keep supported comments hidden and ask you to reload the page.

Outside the scope or not yet verified

Replies, YouTube Studio’s channel-wide and Shorts-specific comment management pages, Super Chat, Super Stickers, and membership notifications are outside the scope.
Some ways comments appear remain unverified, including unfamiliar page layouts and changes in how large numbers of comments are displayed.

Limitations and notes

  • This is a BETA version still in development.
    It is not currently available to the general public through the Chrome Web Store.
  • The extension may hide comments that do not need a pause or miss comments that do.
  • Changes to YouTube’s page structure may prevent the extension from working correctly.
  • The pause is not guaranteed to work for every comment or every way comments appear. Comment text may be visible briefly, including when you first open a page or comments panel.

When you save changes to the settings for your saved words or to your display language, the extension tries to update open, supported comment sections.
Updates may take time or fail to appear on some screens.
If changes do not appear, see how to check for changes in the comments.
